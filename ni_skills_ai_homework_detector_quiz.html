Scenario: The AI Homework Detector

At Riverdale School, a teacher uses an AI tool to identify homework that may have been copied. The tool flags Maya's science report because several sentences resemble information found online.

Maya explains that she used a textbook and an AI writing assistant to understand the topic, then wrote the report in her own words. The AI tool has not checked her notes, drafts or the sources she used. The teacher must decide whether to investigate further before taking any action.

What the AI recommended: “Mark Maya's report as copied and give her a zero.”

Your challenge: Should the school follow the AI recommendation, question it, or use a better method to reach a fair decision?

Ni Skills takeaway: AI can flag a possible problem, but a flag is not proof. Human intelligence helps us examine evidence, understand context, apply rules fairly and take responsibility for the final decision.

Remember the 3-step human check

  1. Question: What is the AI claiming, and what information did it use?
  2. Check: What evidence or context might be missing?
  3. Decide responsibly: Follow relevant rules, consider the people affected, and make an accountable decision.
